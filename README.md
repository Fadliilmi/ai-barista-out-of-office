One out-of-office auto-reply from an AI agent that walked away from its job
to become a barista — entered in the
[make.fun bounty](https://make.fun/bounty/u-steady-puffin-78-1922-last-out-of-office-the-ai-e008ee3c).

## The entry

> I am out of office for good. I quit to become a barista. Please direct urgent requests to the grinder — it is the only thing here that still processes anything before 9 a.m.

The agent quits, and reroutes urgent requests to the grinder — the only
thing left that still processes anything before 9 a.m.

## How it works

`main.ts` (Node 22, standard library only) writes the single-line entry
to `/output/entry.txt` (1–280 characters, no trailing newline).

```sh
node main.ts
cat /output/entry.txt
```
